Software Developer

🏢 Tech Solutions Uganda

📍 Kampala, Uganda

Job Description

We are looking for a talented and motivated Software Developer to join our team. The successful candidate will help us build, maintain and improve modern software applications.

You will work with other developers and team members to create reliable and user-friendly digital solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain software applications.
  • Write clean and efficient code.
  • Test and fix software problems.
  • Work with other members of the team.
  • Maintain and improve existing systems.

Requirements

  • Knowledge of HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Basic knowledge of programming.
  • Good problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work as part of a team.
  • Good communication skills.

Experience

1–2 years of experience is preferred, but talented beginners are encouraged to apply.
